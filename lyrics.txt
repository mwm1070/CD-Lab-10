## Title: **Santa Claus Is Mad in Plymouth, NH**

## Music Description

**Style / genre:** Upbeat traditional Christmas pop with a playful big-band swing feel — sleigh bells, jingle bells, bright trumpets, warm handclaps, acoustic piano, upright bass, brushed drums, glockenspiel, and a children’s choir on the chorus.

**Mood:** Funny and grumpy at first, then warm, grateful, and joyful. Santa is mad because someone didn’t like their presents, but the magical Christmas spirit of Plymouth, NH helps turn the song around.

**Tempo:** About 112–118 BPM, 4/4 time with a light swing shuffle.

**Key:** Verses in G major, with a brighter lift in the chorus and final chorus.

**Vocals:** Lead singer with a theatrical, slightly grumpy Santa-style baritone, supported by cheerful group harmonies and a kids’ choir for the sing-along chorus.

**Structure:** Intro → Verse 1 → Chorus → Verse 2 → Chorus → Verse 3 → Final Chorus → Outro.

---

## Lyrics

### Intro  
Jingle, jingle, bells on snow,  
Santa’s boots about to glow.  
Plymouth, NH, in the night,  
Magic sparkling everywhere.

---

### Verse 1  
A magical place called Plymouth, NH,  
Is glowing with Christmas light.  
Every porch and windowpane  
Is singing through the snowy night.  
Santa landed, sack in hand,  
With starry lists across the land.  
But someone unwrapped presents there,  
And gave a sour, pouting stare.  
“I don’t like these at all,” they said,  
And Santa heard with lowered head.  
Santa Claus, he frowned and sighed,  
“Gratitude is where the joy resides.”

---

### Chorus  
Oh, Santa Claus is mad in Plymouth, NH,  
But Christmas magic unlocks the key.  
When thankfulness fills the air,  
A grateful heart can meet him there.  
Joy is ringing, lights are bright,  
Plymouth, NH makes wrong feel right.  
If a present isn’t your dream,  
Love is better than the frown you mean.

---

### Verse 2  
Santa huffed and tapped his boots,  
His mustache bristled red with snow.  
“I bring the gifts through wind and miles,  
To make your holiday glow.  
In magical Plymouth, NH,  
People there have joy and light;  
Neighbors share their treats and songs,  
They laugh and sing through Christmas night.  
So why roll eyes on Christmas night,  
When everyone is glad to be here?  
Be thankful for what’s in your hand,  
And Santa’s gruffness disappears.”

### Verse 3  
But then the child brought cocoa warm,  
And said, “I’m sorry, Santa Claus,  
I forgot what Christmas is—  
It’s hearts and hands without a pause.  
In magical Plymouth, NH,  
The joy I saw was plain to see:  
Lights on homes and bells in tow,  
Merry thanks from every soul.  
So if the gift don’t meet my wish,  
I’ll thank you for the way you give.”  
Santa laughed and winked his eye,  
“Gratitude keeps the night alive.”

---

### Final Chorus  
Oh, Santa Claus is glad in Plymouth, NH,  
Because the town unlocked the key:  
When thankfulness fills the air,  
A grateful heart can meet him there.  
Joy is ringing, lights are bright,

If a present isn’t your dream,  
Love is better than the frown you mean.

---

### Outro  
Jingle, jingle, bells on snow,  
Santa’s happy, don’t you know.  
Plymouth, NH, so bright tonight,  
Thank-you hearts make Christmas right.
